## Generation prompt

A bold, beautifully balanced continuous repeat loop made from two substantial rounded arrows, enclosing a small right-pointing play triangle in negative space. Flat geometric graphic, mint teal #74dfc4 paired with deep charcoal #202020, matching a dark video-player interface. Compact square silhouette with generous crisp strokes that remain recognizable at 16px. Centered mark occupying approximately 85% of a square canvas, genuinely transparent surrounding background. No presentation board, no mockup, no multiple options, no gradients, no shadows, no hairline details. Entire logo must be original: do not reproduce YouTube's red rounded rectangle or its logo. Deliver one clean high-resolution raster master suitable for resizing into extension icons and use at the top of README documents.
